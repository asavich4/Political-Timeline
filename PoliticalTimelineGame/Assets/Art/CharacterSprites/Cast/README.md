# Character cast

37 individual PNG portraits generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Strong colors, crisp angular silhouettes and simplified Reigns-inspired faces follow the user's hooded-figure reference. The images include their teal card backgrounds. All 182 current cards use this cast, and every cast member appears in the deck.

## Editing in Unity

Open **Political Timeline > Characters > Browse Cast** to see every sprite before Play. Click a portrait to select its PNG in the Project window. Set **Card to edit**, then choose **Use on card** to save a portrait assignment. You can also drag any sprite into a DecisionCard's Portrait field. Assignments support Undo and are not reset on startup.

**Apply Cast to Matching Advisors** reapplies the default role mappings when explicitly selected. **Update Events to New Cast** applies the one-time dialogue and speaker migration; subsequent edits to migrated cards are preserved. The nurse, teacher, farmer, ranger and goose now have matching new artwork. Existing card IDs, effects, policy requirements, follow-ups and saved-game compatibility are retained.

Sprites: `Assets/Art/CharacterSprites/Cast/`. Single-sprite import, bilinear filtering, no mipmaps, uncompressed, 2048 maximum texture size. Import settings remain editable.
