# Character sprite art

The game uses ordinary PNG sprites. Six new characters live in Assets/Art/CharacterSprites/PolicyCharacters.png, imported as a multiple-sprite sheet with six named 512 x 512 cells. Ten existing images from Assets/Sprites are reused. The 102 cards share these characters by role, and each card has an editable Portrait reference.

Created with the built-in image generation tool, using the existing worker, scientist and female politician sprites as style references. No CLI/API fallback was used.

Final generation prompt:

Create a production game sprite sheet with SIX square character portrait tiles arranged EXACTLY in 3 columns and 2 rows, all equally sized, edge-to-edge with no margins, gutters, frames, writing or captions. Landscape overall ratio 3:2. Each tile is a separate complete square game portrait. Use the three supplied images ONLY as style references: match their simple flat illustrated faces, dot or short vertical eyes, restrained rounded silhouettes, muted vintage palette, gentle paper grain, sparse details and bust composition. Simpler than the references; clean appealing individual faces, no awkward jagged polygon meshes, no photorealism, no 3D. Each character fills its own square with head safely below top edge and shoulders reaching bottom. Each tile has its own solid muted background. Top row left: older Black female Supreme Court judge with short silver hair, small glasses, black robe and cream collar, dusty teal background. Top row center: young Latino male campaign organizer with curly dark hair, rolled-up rust shirt and small plain round cream campaign pin, warm sand background. Top row right: middle-aged female farmer with a straw hat, brown braid, dark green overalls and cream shirt, olive background. Bottom row left: Black female nurse with short natural hair, muted blue scrubs and a simple stethoscope, warm ochre background. Bottom row center: middle-aged male teacher with dark swept hair, round glasses, muted brown cardigan, a small closed book at chest, dusty teal background. Bottom row right: older female congressional floor leader with short silver bob, navy jacket and simple cream blouse, burgundy background. No text, no logos, no flags. Keep each portrait completely inside its tile, precise equal grid suitable for slicing directly into six Unity sprites. Match the provided sprite collection closely.

The superseded geometric assets are archived outside the Unity Assets folder in Tools/ArchivedGeometricPortraits. They are not referenced by cards or displayed in the game.
