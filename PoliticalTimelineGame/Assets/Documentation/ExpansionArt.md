# Expansion sprites

Three PNGs generated with the built-in image-generation tool, using PolicyCharacters.png as the style reference. All are imported as normal single Unity sprites in Assets/Art/CharacterSprites:

- Clerk.png: congressional paperwork, hearings and party administration.
- Ranger.png: parks, community work, pollution and disaster readiness.
- Goose.png: the party mascot and its entirely fictional demands.

The existing sprites are reused for the other roles. Assign a card's Portrait field to swap its image. Edit the PNG externally and Unity reimports it. Browse every event in Political Timeline > Portrait Gallery or Content Workshop.

## Final prompts

### Clerk

The reference image is STYLE ONLY: match its simple Reigns-inspired illustrated shapes, rounded restrained faces, tiny dark eyes, soft paper grain, muted warm palette. Subject: a weary middle-aged East Asian male congressional clerk, round spectacles, receding dark hair, rolled-up cream shirtsleeves with dark suspenders, holding a thick stack of papers; muted slate blue background. One portrait only, not a sheet. Centered bust with head comfortably inside edges, shoulders reaching bottom, solid textured background. Flat 2D printed illustration; no text, captions, border, realism, 3D or polygon mesh look. Keep the style consistent with the supplied sprites.
